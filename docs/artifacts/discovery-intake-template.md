# Discovery Intake Form

A structured intake form for capturing product intent before generating discovery artifacts. This is a **human-authored input**, not an AI-generated artifact.

Fill in what you know. Leave unknown sections blank — the generation prompts will mark them as "Not provided."

---

---

## Problem Context

### What is the problem?
- What issue, gap, or unmet need does this initiative address?
- Describe the problem in concrete terms — what goes wrong, what is missing, or what is inefficient?

### Who experiences this problem?
- Which users, user groups, or personas are affected?
- How does each group experience the problem differently?
- Who is most severely affected?

### Why does this matter now?
- What has changed that makes this problem urgent or timely?
- What is the cost of inaction?
- Is there a triggering event, deadline, or strategic shift?

### What evidence do we have?
- What data, research, or observations support this problem's existence?
- How was this problem identified (user feedback, metrics, competitive analysis, internal observation)?
- What is the confidence level in this evidence?

---

## Users and Stakeholders

### Primary users
- Who will directly benefit from solving this problem?
- What are their key characteristics relevant to this problem?
- What do they currently do to cope with this problem?

### Secondary users
- Who is indirectly affected?
- Are there users with conflicting needs?

### Sponsor
- Who is the business sponsor or decision-maker for this initiative?

---

## Opportunity

### How big is this opportunity?
- What is the estimated impact of solving this problem? (revenue, cost savings, user growth, efficiency gains)
- What data supports this estimate?
- What is uncertain about this estimate?

### Strategic alignment
- Which organizational strategy or objectives does this support?
- How does this fit into the current product roadmap?

---

## Current State

### How is the problem handled today?
- What existing solutions, workarounds, or manual processes exist?
- What tools or systems are currently involved?

### What has been tried before?
- Have there been previous attempts to solve this problem?
- What happened and why was it insufficient?

### Existing system context (if applicable)
- For initiatives involving existing systems: describe the system, its current capabilities, and its limitations
- What technical, data, or integration context is relevant?

---

## Scope and Boundaries

### What is in scope?
- What aspects of the problem should this initiative address?

### What is explicitly out of scope?
- What related problems or features are intentionally excluded?
- Why are they excluded?

### Known constraints
- Are there regulatory, compliance, or legal constraints?
- Are there budgetary or resource constraints?
- Are there timeline constraints or deadlines?
- Are there technical constraints from existing systems?

---

## Assumptions and Risks

### What are we assuming to be true?
- List any conditions assumed to be true that, if false, would change the direction of this initiative

### What could go wrong?
- What risks or blockers could prevent progress?
- Are there unresolved decisions that could affect scope?

---

## Additional Context

### Reference documents
- Links to relevant background material, research, prior discussions

### Related initiatives
- Are there other in-flight or planned initiatives that relate to this problem?
- Are there dependencies or sequencing concerns?

---

## Completeness Checklist

Before using this intake to generate discovery artifacts, confirm:
- [ ] Problem is clearly described in concrete terms
- [ ] At least one affected user group is identified
- [ ] "Why now" rationale is provided
- [ ] Some evidence basis exists (even if limited)
- [ ] Scope boundaries are stated (in scope and out of scope)
- [ ] Known constraints are listed
