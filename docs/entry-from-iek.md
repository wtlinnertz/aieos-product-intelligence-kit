# Entry from Insight & Evolution Kit (IEK)

**You are here because:** An Evolution Signal (ES) from IEK has returned a re-entry signal of `re-discover`, with a specific discovery question in §6 and recommended actions in §7.

**What you must bring:**

| Artifact | Status Required | Where to Find It |
|----------|----------------|-----------------|
| Evolution Signal (ES-{SCOPE}-{NNN}) — §6 and §7 must be complete | Frozen | IEK `docs/artifacts/` in the consuming project |
| Discovery question from ES §6 | N/A — extracted from ES | ES §6 Re-Discovery Question |
| Engagement Record (ER-{INITIATIVE}-{NNN}) from the previous initiative | Frozen (optional) | `docs/engagement/` in the consuming project |

**First artifact to generate in this kit:** Work Classification Record (WCR) — to classify the new initiative type before re-entering the discovery flow

**Where to start:** `docs/session-setup.md` → "Work Classification Record (WCR)"

**What changes at this boundary:**

- You are starting a new discovery engagement, not continuing the previous one. The ES surfaces a question; PIK answers it from first principles.
- The scope of the new engagement is defined by the ES §6 discovery question — not by the previous initiative's scope. The new PFD must be bounded by the new question.
- Evidence from the previous initiative (RHRs, ERs, PMRs) is available as input — use it in the Discovery Intake Form and AR as supporting evidence, not as a starting constraint.

**Common mistakes at this transition:**

| Mistake | How to Avoid |
|---------|--------------|
| Reusing the previous PFD and patching it for the new question | Start a fresh PFD. The `re-discover` signal means the previous problem framing is no longer sufficient. |
| Treating the ES re-entry as automatic authorization to build | The WCR and Discovery Intake must pass their respective validators before discovery begins. Re-entry is not a bypass. |
| Skipping the WCR because "we know what we're doing" | The WCR is required to classify the incoming work and confirm the routing decision before discovery starts. |

**If you arrived here without a re-discover signal:**

If the ES signal is `maintain` or `watch`, PIK re-entry is not indicated. Return to IEK and act on the ES recommended actions. `watch` means continue operating and monitoring; it does not trigger discovery. Only a `re-discover` signal with a complete ES §6 discovery question justifies entering PIK from IEK.
