# Entry from Strategic Direction Kit (SDK)

## Boundary Briefing

You are entering the Product Intelligence Kit from the Strategic Direction Kit (Layer 1). This document describes what you bring, what this kit expects, and what happens next.

---

## What You Bring

- A frozen Portfolio Prioritization Record (PPR) identifying above-the-line bets
- One or more frozen Strategic Bet Records (SBRs) for the funded bets
- Strategic context: thesis, success/failure signals, investment envelope, time horizon
- Optional: frozen Product Capability Roadmap (PCR) — provides broader product context for the specific bet being discovered

## What This Kit Expects

PIK expects the strategic bet to be frozen, falsifiable, and prioritized. The SBR provides the "why" and the "what success looks like." PIK's job is to structure the "what should we build" through discovery.

**The SBR is not a product brief.** It does not define features, user stories, or acceptance criteria. It defines a strategic thesis and measurable signals. PIK's Discovery Intake and PFD translate the strategic bet into a structured problem framing.

## What Happens Here

1. Use the SBR thesis (§2) and signals (§3, §4) as strategic context when filling the Discovery Intake Form
2. Reference the PPR priority position and investment envelope as resource constraints
3. Proceed through the standard PIK flow: Discovery Intake → PFD → VH → AR → EL → DPRD
4. The DPRD traces back to the SBR through the Discovery Intake's strategic context section
